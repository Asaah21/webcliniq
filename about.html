About

A nurse who learned to build and secure websites, now working only with healthcare practices.

I'm Emmanuel Asaah Alemya. I spent years on the floor as a registered nurse, then taught myself to build software, alongside a background in security and technical support. WebCliniQ is my own business, not an agency. When you message it, you're talking to me.

Each part of that background shapes the work. From nursing, I know how a patient picks a practice: the Google reviews and the listing, long before they see your homepage. From building, I can make the fix myself instead of managing someone who does. From security and support, I'm careful about what your forms and review replies can expose, which matters more in healthcare than in most fields.

Most of the work is small and specific: a slow phone site, a thin listing, no recent reviews. Most practices need three or four fixes, and I only suggest a rebuild if the audit clearly points there.

A small bench of vetted subcontractors covers jobs that need extra hands. You still deal with one person: me.

Reach me directly at support@webcliniq.com or on LinkedIn.
