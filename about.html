About

A nurse who learned to build and secure the web.

I'm Emmanuel Asaah Alemya. I spent years on the floor as a registered nurse, then taught myself to build software, alongside a background in security and technical support. WebCliniQ is my own practice — not an agency. When you message it, you're talking to me.

Those three strands are the whole point. From nursing, I know how a patient actually picks a practice: the Google reviews and the listing, long before they see your homepage. From building, I can do the fix myself instead of managing someone who does. From security and support, I'm careful about what your forms and review replies quietly expose — which matters more in healthcare than most fields.

The work is aimed at the small things that move patient numbers — a slow phone site, a thin listing, no recent reviews — not big rebuilds. Most practices need three or four specific fixes, and a rebuild only comes up if the audit genuinely points there.

A small bench of vetted subcontractors covers jobs that need extra hands. You still deal with one person: me.

Reach me directly at support@webcliniq.com or on LinkedIn.
