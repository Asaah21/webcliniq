About

Run by a nurse, not an account manager

WebCliniQ is one person — a registered nurse who taught himself to build software, working with a small bench of vetted subcontractors for the jobs that need extra hands. When you message WebCliniQ, you're talking to the person actually doing the work, not a rotating account manager.

The idea came from a simple observation: most dental practices don't need a full website rebuild. They need three or four specific, fixable things — slow load times, a thin Google listing, no recent reviews — the quiet stuff that costs them a new patient before that patient ever picks up the phone.

WebCliniQ starts with a free, real audit of exactly that. Most practices only need the fixes it turns up. A full rebuild only gets suggested when the audit actually points there — never as the first pitch.

Why a nurse

Dental fluency, not guesswork

  • Knows a patient picks a dental practice off your Google reviews before they ever see your homepage
  • Understands what actually builds trust for a patient booking online, not just what looks good in a portfolio
  • Careful with what review responses and website forms reveal — patient trust matters online, too
  • Direct access to the person doing the work — no handoffs, no account managers
