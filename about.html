About

Run by a nurse, not an account manager

I'm Emmanuel Asaah Alemya, a registered nurse who taught himself to build software. WebCliniQ is my own practice, not an agency. I bring in a small bench of vetted subcontractors only for jobs that need extra hands. When you message WebCliniQ, you're talking to me, not a rotating account manager.

The idea came from a simple observation: most healthcare practices don't need a full website rebuild. They need three or four specific, fixable things. Slow load times, a thin Google listing, no recent reviews. The quiet stuff that costs a new patient before they ever pick up the phone.

WebCliniQ starts with a free, real audit of exactly that. Most practices only need the fixes it turns up. A full rebuild only gets suggested when the audit actually points there, never as the first pitch.

Reach me directly at support@webcliniq.com or on LinkedIn.

Why a nurse

Healthcare fluency, not guesswork

  • Knows people pick a healthcare provider off Google reviews before they ever see your homepage
  • Understands what actually builds trust for someone booking care online, not just what looks good in a portfolio
  • Careful with what review responses and website forms reveal. Patient trust matters online, too
  • Direct access to the person doing the work. No handoffs, no account managers
