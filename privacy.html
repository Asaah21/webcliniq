Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This page explains what WebCliniQ collects when you use the free audit tool or contact us, and what we do with it.

What we collect from the audit tool

When you run an audit, we collect the business name and/or website you enter, the findings the audit produces, and your IP address (used only to prevent abuse of the tool, see below). We do not ask for or collect any patient information, and the audit tool never touches or reads anything behind a login on your website.

How we use Google's APIs

The audit calls Google's PageSpeed Insights API (to check your site speed and technical health) and Google's Places API (to check your public Google Business Profile). Both only read information that is already public. We do not request access to your Google account, analytics, or any private dashboard as part of the free audit.

Why we log your IP address

We keep a short record of IP addresses tied to audit requests solely to prevent automated abuse of the free tool. We don't use this to identify or track visitors for any other purpose.

Contact form and direct messages

If you reach out via the contact form, WhatsApp, or email, we keep that information (name, email, message) only to respond to you and, if you become a client, to deliver the work you've asked for.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your information to third parties, and we don't run ads that use your data to target you.
