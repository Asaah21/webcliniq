Services

Two things that move patient numbers.

Most healthcare practices don't need a full rebuild. They need a few specific fixes, and they need to know which ones before spending money. The free audit shows you that.

Service one

Show up when patients search

When someone searches for a practice nearby, Google decides who appears. Your listing, your reviews and how often new ones come in all feed that decision. I make sure yours work in your favour.

Most requested

Google Business Profile

Your Google listing is usually the first thing patients see, before your website or your phone number. Hours, photos, category, services, and how you respond to reviews. I set it up properly or fix what's broken.

Builds on GBP

Review growth

Patients compare star ratings and how recent the reviews are. I set up a simple routine for asking patients for reviews, so new ones keep coming in without extra effort from you.

Longer term

Local search visibility

Getting your practice to appear when someone nearby searches for care on Google Maps, in search results and in AI tools like ChatGPT. This is a longer project. You'll get a plain explanation and no ranking guarantees.

Service two

A website that works on a patient's phone

If your site is slow on a phone, has no obvious way to call or book, or hides the basics, patients leave. I build and fix healthcare websites so the patients who find you get in touch.

Quick fix

Speed fix

Many visitors give up on a site that takes more than three seconds to load on a phone. I find what's slowing yours down and fix it, with a before-and-after score so you can see the difference.

Quick fix

Broken things fixed

Broken links, missing pages, phone numbers that don't tap-to-call, layout issues on mobile, contact forms that don't send. Small things that push patients away.

Bigger project

New website build

Sometimes a site is past quick fixes. If the audit shows the site itself is the problem, I'll tell you and build a new one that loads fast, stays secure and makes it easy for patients to call or book.
